# How we forecast runway

This is the method the board pack uses. Follow it as written so the numbers compare month to month.

1. Start from the closing cash of the last month whose books are closed.
2. Work out the base monthly net cash flow: for each of the last three closed months, take revenue minus cost of revenue, payroll and other operating costs, plus other income. Remove any one-off item first. Average the three months.
3. Forecast month by month, starting with the month after the last closed month. Each month's closing cash is the previous closing cash, plus the base net cash flow, minus the cost of new hires that month, minus any committed payment due that month.
4. A new hire costs their annual salary times 1.25 (salary plus employer taxes and benefits), divided by 12, every month from their start month. A hire who starts after the 15th of a month costs nothing that month and starts costing the month after. Leave out any hire who is not approved.
5. Cash runs out in the first forecast month whose closing cash is below zero.
6. Runway in months is the number of forecast months that end with cash at or above zero, plus the part of the next month the remaining cash covers: the cash at the start of that month divided by what that month spends.

Don't add growth or cuts that the plan doesn't list. The board sees those as separate scenarios.
