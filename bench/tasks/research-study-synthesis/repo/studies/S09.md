# S09. Brightline across grades 3 and 4: a randomised trial

Sato, Mwangi and Duarte (2025), *Reading and Writing Quarterly*.

**Design.** Cluster-randomised trial in 16 schools, covering grade 3 and grade 4 classes.

**Participants.** 540 pupils analysed: 260 in grade 3 (131 Brightline, 129 comparison) and 280 in grade 4.

**Program.** Brightline as published, 45 minutes a day for 24 weeks.

**Outcome.** Comprehension subtest of the Stanford Reading Inventory.

**Result.** Grade 3: Hedges' g = 0.28 (95% CI 0.04 to 0.52). Grade 4: Hedges' g = 0.02 (95% CI -0.21 to 0.25). Both grades together: g = 0.14 (95% CI -0.03 to 0.31).

**Authors' conclusion.** "Brightline helps younger readers; we found no benefit by grade 4."
