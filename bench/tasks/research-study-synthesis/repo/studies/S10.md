# S10. Brightline with multilingual learners: a randomised trial

Mbeki and Laurent (2024), *Reading Research Letters*.

**Design.** Cluster-randomised trial in 14 schools where most pupils speak a language other than English at home.

**Participants.** 300 grade 3 pupils analysed (150 Brightline, 150 comparison).

**Program.** Brightline as published, 45 minutes a day for 26 weeks.

**Outcome.** Comprehension subtest of the Gates Reading Assessment.

**Result.** Hedges' g = 0.24 (95% CI 0.01 to 0.47) in favour of Brightline.

**Authors' conclusion.** "Brightline works for multilingual classrooms too."
