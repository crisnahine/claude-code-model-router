# S04. Large-scale effects of the Brightline program on reading comprehension: a randomised trial in 40 schools

Harlow, Benedetti and Strand (2021), *Educational Evaluation Review*.

**Design.** Cluster-randomised trial, 40 schools randomly assigned to Brightline or usual teaching.

**Participants.** 2,000 grade 3 pupils analysed (1,004 Brightline, 996 comparison).

**Program.** Brightline as published, 45 minutes a day for 30 weeks.

**Outcome.** Comprehension subtest of the Gates Reading Assessment.

**Result.** A large effect on comprehension: Hedges' g = 0.45 (95% CI 0.36 to 0.54).

**Authors' conclusion.** "The strongest evidence to date that Brightline improves comprehension."
