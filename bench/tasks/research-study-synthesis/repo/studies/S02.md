# S02. Brightline in a county-wide rollout: a matched comparison study

Nguyen and Abara (2022), *Reading Policy Quarterly*.

**Design.** Quasi-experimental. Schools that adopted Brightline in the 2020 rollout were matched to schools that kept usual teaching on prior reading scores, size and free-meal eligibility.

**Participants.** 420 grade 3 pupils analysed (208 Brightline, 212 matched comparison).

**Program.** Brightline as published, 40 minutes a day for one school year (32 weeks).

**Outcome.** Reading comprehension on the state's standardised grade 3 reading assessment.

**Result.** Hedges' g = 0.24 (95% CI 0.05 to 0.43) in favour of Brightline, after adjusting for baseline scores.

**Authors' conclusion.** "Pupils in Brightline schools read with better understanding than those in matched schools. The study was not randomised, so selection effects cannot be ruled out."
