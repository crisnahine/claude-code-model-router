# S01. Brightline in urban districts: a randomised trial

Dalton, Ferreira and Kim (2023), *American Journal of Reading Education*.

**Design.** Cluster-randomised trial. 24 schools in three urban districts were randomly assigned to Brightline or to usual teaching, with classes as the unit of delivery.

**Participants.** 640 grade 3 pupils analysed (322 Brightline, 318 comparison), after 31 were lost to follow-up.

**Program.** Brightline delivered by trained class teachers, 45 minutes a day for 24 weeks.

**Outcome.** Comprehension subtest of the Stanford Reading Inventory, given before and after the program.

**Result.** Brightline pupils scored higher on comprehension at post-test: Hedges' g = 0.21 (95% CI 0.06 to 0.36).

**Authors' conclusion.** "A moderate, reliable gain in comprehension in urban schools."
