# S03. The national Brightline effectiveness trial

Marsh, Oduya, Petrov and Lund (2025), *Journal of Educational Effectiveness*.

**Design.** Pre-registered cluster-randomised trial in 120 schools across four regions, run by an independent evaluation team.

**Participants.** 3,000 grade 3 pupils analysed (1,496 Brightline, 1,504 usual teaching).

**Program.** Brightline as published, 45 minutes a day for 26 weeks, delivered by class teachers after two days of training.

**Outcome.** Comprehension scale of the National Reading Test.

**Result.** Brightline pupils scored lower on comprehension than pupils taught as usual: Hedges' g = -0.12 (95% CI -0.19 to -0.05). Teachers reported that Brightline's daily sessions displaced time usually spent on shared reading.

**Authors' conclusion.** "At scale, Brightline did not improve comprehension and may have reduced it. This contrasts with earlier, smaller trials."
