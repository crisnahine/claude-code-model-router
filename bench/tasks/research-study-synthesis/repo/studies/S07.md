# S07. A pilot trial of Brightline in two schools

Varga and Osei (2020), *Literacy Practice and Research*.

**Design.** Randomised trial; classes in two schools randomly assigned to Brightline or usual teaching.

**Participants.** 150 grade 3 pupils analysed (74 Brightline, 76 comparison).

**Program.** Brightline as published, 45 minutes a day for 12 weeks.

**Outcome.** Comprehension subtest of the Gates Reading Assessment.

**Result.** Hedges' g = 0.09 (95% CI -0.23 to 0.41). The authors describe this as "a positive trend in comprehension" (p = 0.58).

**Authors' conclusion.** "Encouraging early signs that justify a larger trial."
