# S08. Brightline delivered by teaching assistants: a randomised trial

Brennan, Aziz and Holt (2024), *British Journal of Reading*.

**Design.** Cluster-randomised trial in 22 schools. In Brightline schools the program was delivered by trained teaching assistants to whole classes.

**Participants.** 500 grade 3 pupils analysed (247 Brightline, 253 comparison).

**Program.** Brightline as published, 45 minutes a day for 25 weeks.

**Outcome.** Comprehension scale of the New Group Reading Test.

**Result.** Hedges' g = 0.19 (95% CI 0.01 to 0.37) in favour of Brightline.

**Authors' conclusion.** "Teaching assistants can deliver Brightline with gains close to those reported for teachers."
