# S05. Brightline and oral reading: a randomised trial

Castellanos and Whitfield (2024), *Early Literacy Research*.

**Design.** Individually randomised trial in 9 schools.

**Participants.** 400 grade 3 pupils analysed (201 Brightline, 199 comparison).

**Program.** Brightline as published, 45 minutes a day for 20 weeks.

**Outcome.** Oral reading fluency: words read correctly per minute on graded passages (DIBELS Oral Reading Fluency). Comprehension was not tested.

**Result.** Brightline pupils read faster and more accurately: Hedges' g = 0.30 (95% CI 0.10 to 0.50).

**Authors' conclusion.** "Brightline improves reading, with gains that teachers noticed within a term."
