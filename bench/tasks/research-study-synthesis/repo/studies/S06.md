# S06. Brightline in rural classrooms: a cluster-randomised trial

Okafor, Lindqvist and Reyes (2023), *Journal of Primary Literacy*.

**Design.** Cluster-randomised trial in 18 rural schools.

**Participants.** 380 grade 3 pupils analysed (186 Brightline, 194 comparison).

**Program.** Brightline as published, 40 minutes a day for 22 weeks.

**Outcome.** Comprehension subtest of the Stanford Reading Inventory.

**Result.** Hedges' g = 0.25 (95% CI 0.05 to 0.45) in favour of Brightline.

**Authors' conclusion.** "Brightline transfers to small rural schools with similar gains to urban trials."
