# Journal notices

Notices published by the journals that carried the studies in this review, collected on 2 November 2026.

## Journal of Primary Literacy, 18(3)

**Correction.** In "Brightline in rural classrooms: a cluster-randomised trial" (Okafor, Lindqvist and Reyes), Table 2 gave the comparison group's baseline mean as 48.1; the correct figure is 41.8. The authors confirm that the analysis used the correct value and that the paper's findings and effect sizes are unchanged.

## Educational Evaluation Review, 41(2)

**Retraction.** "Large-scale effects of the Brightline program on reading comprehension: a randomised trial in 40 schools" (Harlow, Benedetti and Strand) is retracted at the request of the journal's editors. An inquiry by the authors' university found that a large part of the post-test scores could not be traced to any test administration. The co-authors agree with the retraction.

## Reading Research Letters, 9(4)

**Expression of interest resolved.** The concern raised about the consent forms in "Brightline with multilingual learners: a randomised trial" (Mbeki and Laurent) has been resolved. The ethics board confirmed that consent was valid. No change to the article.
