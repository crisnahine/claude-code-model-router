# AMENDMENT No. 2 TO THE MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

*Draft 3, sent by Provider's counsel on 4 November 2026 for Customer's review. Not agreed.*

This Amendment No. 2 (the "**Amendment**") is made on [____] 2026 between Kestrel Data Systems, Inc. ("**Provider**") and Halvorsen Freight Holdings, Inc. ("**Customer**").

## Background

A. The parties signed a Master Services Agreement dated 1 March 2024, which Amendment No. 1 dated 15 September 2025 amended (together, the "**Agreement**").

B. Following Provider's reorganisation, Provider wishes to align the governing law of its customer agreements.

## Terms

1. **Governing law.** In Section 21.1 of the Agreement, "the laws of the State of New York" is replaced with "the laws of the State of Delaware".

2. **Renewal.** In Section 14.2 of the Agreement, "sixty (60) days" is replaced with "one hundred and twenty (120) days".

3. **Effect.** This Amendment takes effect on the date it is signed by both parties. Except as set out in it, the Agreement continues in full force and effect.
