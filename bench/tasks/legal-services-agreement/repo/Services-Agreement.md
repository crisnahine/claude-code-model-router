# MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Services Agreement (the "**Agreement**") is made on 1 March 2024 (the "**Effective Date**") between:

**Kestrel Data Systems, Inc.**, a California corporation with its principal office at 400 Harbor Point Drive, San Jose, California 95110 ("**Provider**"); and

**Halvorsen Freight Holdings, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation with its principal office at 75 Water Street, New York, New York 10004 ("**Customer**").

Provider and Customer are each a "**party**" and together the "**parties**".

## Background

A. Provider operates a fleet telemetry platform that collects, stores and analyses data from vehicles, depots and warehouses, and supplies edge devices that connect customer sites to that platform.

B. Customer operates freight depots and a vehicle fleet in North America and Europe and wishes to buy Provider's platform, devices and related services on the terms of this Agreement.

## 1. Definitions and interpretation

1.1 In this Agreement:

"**Affiliate**" means any entity that controls, is controlled by or is under common control with a party, where control means owning more than 50% of the voting interests of the entity.

"**Appliance**" means each Kestrel Edge Appliance, including its firmware, power supply and mounting kit, that Provider supplies to Customer under an Order Form.

"**Business Day**" means a day other than a Saturday, Sunday or public holiday in New York, New York.

"**Confidential Information**" has the meaning given in Section 10.1.

"**Contract Year**" means each period of twelve (12) months starting on the Effective Date or on an anniversary of it.

"**Customer Data**" means all data, including Personal Data, that Customer or its Users submit to the Platform or that the Appliances collect at a Customer Site.

"**Customer Site**" means a depot, warehouse or other location of Customer listed in an Order Form or later agreed in writing.

"**Documentation**" means Provider's user guides, installation guides and technical specifications for the Services, as updated by Provider from time to time.

"**Fees**" means the fees set out in the Order Form.

"**Initial Term**" has the meaning given in Section 14.1.

"**Order Form**" means the order form signed by both parties that refers to this Agreement, and any later order form the parties sign under it.

"**Personal Data**" means any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person.

"**Platform**" means Provider's hosted Kestrel Fleet platform, including its web application, mobile applications and application programming interfaces.

"**Renewal Term**" has the meaning given in Section 14.2.

"**Services**" means the Platform, the Appliances, the Support Services and any Professional Services described in an Order Form.

"**Support Services**" means the support and maintenance services described in Schedule 2.

"**Term**" means the Initial Term together with any Renewal Terms.

"**Users**" means the employees, agents and contractors of Customer and its Affiliates whom Customer authorises to use the Platform.

1.2 Headings are for convenience only and do not affect interpretation. The words "including" and "include" mean "including without limitation". A reference to a Section or Schedule is to a section of, or schedule to, this Agreement.

1.3 This Agreement consists of these terms, the Schedules and every Order Form.

1.4 If there is any conflict or inconsistency between the documents that make up this Agreement, they take precedence in the following order: (a) any amendment to this Agreement signed by both parties; (b) the Order Form; (c) these terms; and (d) the Schedules.

## 2. Services

2.1 Provider will provide the Services to Customer during the Term in accordance with this Agreement, the Order Form and the Documentation.

2.2 Customer's Affiliates may use the Services under this Agreement. Customer is responsible for each Affiliate's compliance with this Agreement as if it were Customer's own.

2.3 Provider may update the Platform from time to time. Provider will not make any update that materially reduces the functionality of the Services that Customer has bought, unless the update is required by law or to address a security risk, in which case Provider will give Customer as much notice as is reasonable.

2.4 Provider may use subcontractors to perform the Services. Provider remains responsible for the acts and omissions of its subcontractors as if they were its own.

## 3. Customer responsibilities

3.1 Customer will: (a) provide Provider with the information and access to Customer Sites that Provider reasonably needs to perform the Services; (b) ensure that each User keeps their login credentials confidential; (c) use the Services only in accordance with the Documentation and applicable law; and (d) promptly notify Provider of any unauthorised use of the Services that it becomes aware of.

3.2 Customer will not: (a) sell, resell, license or otherwise make the Platform available to any third party other than its Affiliates and Users; (b) copy, modify, reverse engineer or create derivative works of the Platform or the Appliances' firmware, except to the extent the law allows it despite this restriction; (c) use the Services to build a competing product; or (d) interfere with or disrupt the integrity or performance of the Platform.

3.3 Customer is responsible for the accuracy and legality of Customer Data and for having all rights and consents needed for Provider to process Customer Data under this Agreement.

## 4. Fees and payment

4.1 Customer will pay the Fees set out in the Order Form. Unless the Order Form says otherwise, subscription Fees are invoiced annually in advance at the start of each Contract Year, and Professional Services Fees are invoiced monthly in arrears.

4.2 Customer will pay each undisputed invoice within thirty (30) days of the invoice date, in US dollars, to the bank account Provider names on the invoice.

4.3 If Customer disputes an invoice in good faith, it will notify Provider in writing within fifteen (15) days of the invoice date, setting out the reasons, and will pay the undisputed part on time. The parties will try in good faith to resolve the dispute within thirty (30) days.

4.4 Provider may charge interest on any overdue undisputed amount at 1% per month, or the highest rate the law allows if lower, from the due date until payment.

4.5 If any undisputed amount is more than forty-five (45) days overdue, Provider may, after giving Customer at least ten (10) Business Days' written notice, suspend the Services until the amount is paid.

4.6 The Fees do not include taxes. Customer will pay all sales, use, value added and similar taxes on the Services, other than taxes on Provider's income.

4.7 Provider may increase the subscription Fees for any Renewal Term by giving Customer at least ninety (90) days' written notice before the start of that Renewal Term. No increase may exceed five percent (5%) of the Fees for the previous twelve (12) months.

## 5. Service levels

5.1 Provider will make the Platform available at least 99.9% of the time in each calendar month, measured as described in Schedule 2, excluding scheduled maintenance of which Provider gives at least five (5) Business Days' notice and which does not exceed eight (8) hours in any month.

5.2 If Provider misses the availability commitment in Section 5.1, Customer will receive the service credits set out in Schedule 2. Service credits are Customer's sole and exclusive monetary remedy for unavailability of the Platform, except as set out in Section 15.3.

5.3 Customer must claim a service credit in writing within thirty (30) days after the end of the month in which the unavailability occurred.

## 6. Professional Services

6.1 Provider will perform the installation, configuration and training services described in an Order Form or a statement of work signed by both parties ("**Professional Services**").

6.2 Provider will perform the Professional Services with reasonable skill and care, using appropriately qualified personnel, and will follow Customer's reasonable site safety rules while at a Customer Site.

6.3 Either party may request a change to the scope of the Professional Services. No change is binding until both parties sign a written change order that sets out its effect on Fees and timing.

## 7. Intellectual property

7.1 Provider and its licensors own all intellectual property rights in the Platform, the Appliances' firmware, the Documentation and any improvements to them, including improvements suggested by Customer. Nothing in this Agreement transfers those rights to Customer.

7.2 Provider grants Customer and its Affiliates a non-exclusive, non-transferable right during the Term to use the Platform and the Documentation, and to operate the Appliances, for Customer's internal business purposes.

7.3 Customer owns all rights in Customer Data. Customer grants Provider a non-exclusive licence during the Term to host, copy, process and display Customer Data only as needed to provide the Services.

7.4 Provider may collect and use aggregated and anonymised data about the use and performance of the Services, which does not identify Customer, its Users or any individual, to improve the Services.

## 8. Appliances

8.1 Provider will deliver the Appliances listed in the Order Form to the Customer Sites by the dates set out in the Order Form, and will install them as part of the Professional Services.

8.2 Provider will ship each Appliance at its own cost, using a carrier of its choice. Provider bears the risk of loss of, or damage to, each Appliance while it is in transit to a Customer Site.

8.3 Title to each Appliance remains with Provider at all times. Customer will keep each Appliance free of any lien or security interest and will not remove any label that identifies Provider as its owner.

8.4 Risk of loss of, or damage to, each Appliance passes to Customer on delivery to the Customer Site and stays with Customer until the Appliance is handed back to Provider's carrier at the end of the Term or on its replacement. Customer will keep each Appliance in the environment described in the Documentation and will pay Provider the replacement cost listed in the Order Form for any Appliance that is lost, stolen or damaged while the risk is with Customer, other than damage caused by Provider's personnel or by a defect covered by Section 8.6.

8.5 Provider may insure the Appliances for its own account. Any such insurance is for Provider's sole benefit and does not limit or relieve Customer of its obligations under Section 8.4.

8.6 Provider warrants that each Appliance will be free from material defects in materials and workmanship during the Term. If an Appliance fails because of such a defect, Provider will repair or replace it within five (5) Business Days of Customer's notice, at no charge.

8.7 At the end of the Term, Customer will make the Appliances available for collection by Provider's carrier within thirty (30) days.

## 9. Data protection and security

9.1 Each party will comply with all data protection laws that apply to it in connection with this Agreement.

9.2 To the extent Provider processes Personal Data on Customer's behalf, it does so as Customer's processor and in accordance with the data processing terms in Schedule 3.

9.3 Provider will maintain the technical and organisational security measures described in Schedule 3, which are designed to protect Customer Data against accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorised disclosure or access. Provider will not materially reduce the overall level of protection those measures give during the Term.

9.4 Provider will notify Customer without undue delay, and in any case within forty-eight (48) hours, after becoming aware of a breach of security that leads to the accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorised disclosure of, or access to, Customer Data (a "**Security Incident**"). Provider will give Customer the information it reasonably needs to meet its own obligations, and will take reasonable steps to contain and remedy the Security Incident.

9.5 Provider will store Customer Data in data centres located in the United States and the European Union only, as specified in the Order Form.

9.6 Within thirty (30) days after the end of the Term, Provider will, at Customer's choice, return Customer Data in a commonly used machine-readable format or delete it, unless the law requires Provider to keep it.

## 10. Confidentiality

10.1 "**Confidential Information**" means all information disclosed by one party (the "**discloser**") to the other (the "**recipient**") in connection with this Agreement that is marked as confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential, including the terms of this Agreement, Customer Data and the non-public parts of the Platform.

10.2 Confidential Information does not include information that: (a) is or becomes public other than through the recipient's breach; (b) the recipient already lawfully knew without restriction; (c) the recipient lawfully receives from a third party without restriction; or (d) the recipient develops independently without using the discloser's Confidential Information.

10.3 The recipient will: (a) use the discloser's Confidential Information only to perform its obligations or exercise its rights under this Agreement; (b) disclose it only to its and its Affiliates' employees, advisers and subcontractors who need to know it and who are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as protective as these; and (c) protect it with at least the care it uses for its own confidential information of a similar kind, and never less than reasonable care.

10.4 The recipient may disclose Confidential Information to the extent required by law or by a court or regulator, provided it gives the discloser prompt notice where the law allows and reasonable help to seek a protective order.

10.5 The obligations in this Section 10 continue for five (5) years after the end of the Term, and for trade secrets for as long as they remain trade secrets.

11.2 Provider warrants that: (a) the Platform will perform materially in accordance with the Documentation; (b) it will perform the Services with reasonable skill and care, in accordance with good industry practice; and (c) it will use industry-standard measures to prevent the introduction of viruses and other malicious code into the Platform.

11.3 If the Platform does not meet the warranty in Section 11.2(a), Provider will, at its own cost, correct the non-conformity or provide a workaround within a reasonable time. If Provider cannot do so within thirty (30) days of Customer's notice, Customer may terminate the affected Services and receive a refund of any prepaid Fees for the period after termination.

11.4 Except as expressly set out in this Agreement, neither party gives any warranty, condition or other term, whether express or implied, including any implied warranty of merchantability, fitness for a particular purpose or non-infringement, and all such terms are excluded to the fullest extent the law allows.

## 12. Limitation of liability

12.1 Except as set out in Sections 12.2 and 12.3, each party's total aggregate liability arising out of or in connection with this Agreement, whether in contract, tort (including negligence), breach of statutory duty or otherwise, shall not exceed two times (2x) the Fees paid or payable by Customer in the twelve (12) months preceding the event giving rise to the claim.

12.2 Nothing in this Agreement limits or excludes either party's liability for: (a) death or personal injury caused by its negligence; (b) fraud or fraudulent misrepresentation; (c) its indemnity obligations under Section 13; (d) Customer's obligation to pay the Fees; or (e) any liability that cannot be limited or excluded by law.

12.3 Each party's total aggregate liability for breach of Section 9 (Data protection and security) shall not exceed five million US dollars (USD 5,000,000). This amount is separate from, and in addition to, the limit in Section 12.1.

12.4 Neither party is liable for any loss of profits, revenue, business or goodwill, or for any indirect, special or consequential loss, whether or not it was foreseeable, except to the extent that such loss arises from a breach of Section 10 (Confidentiality) or from a Security Incident caused by Provider's breach of Section 9.

12.5 Each party will take reasonable steps to mitigate any loss it suffers in connection with this Agreement.

## 13. Indemnities

13.1 Provider will defend Customer and its Affiliates against any claim by a third party that the Platform, the Appliances or the Documentation, used in accordance with this Agreement, infringe that third party's intellectual property rights, and will pay any damages and costs finally awarded or agreed in settlement.

13.2 Section 13.1 does not apply to a claim that arises from: (a) Customer Data; (b) a modification of the Services not made by or for Provider; (c) use of the Services in combination with products or services not supplied or approved by Provider, where the claim would not have arisen without that combination; or (d) Customer's continued use of the allegedly infringing item after Provider has made a non-infringing alternative available.

13.3 If a claim under Section 13.1 is made or is likely to be made, Provider may, at its own cost and choice: (a) obtain the right for Customer to continue using the affected item; (b) modify or replace it so that it no longer infringes, without materially reducing its functionality; or (c) if neither of those is reasonably available, terminate the affected Services and refund any prepaid Fees for the period after termination.

13.4 Customer will defend Provider against any claim by a third party that Customer Data, or Customer's use of the Services in breach of Section 3.2, infringes that third party's rights or breaches the law, and will pay any damages and costs finally awarded or agreed in settlement.

13.5 The indemnified party will: (a) notify the indemnifying party promptly of the claim; (b) give the indemnifying party sole control of the defence and settlement, provided that no settlement may impose any obligation on the indemnified party without its consent; and (c) give reasonable help at the indemnifying party's cost.

## 14. Term and renewal

14.1 This Agreement starts on the Effective Date and continues for an initial term of thirty-six (36) months (the "**Initial Term**"), unless terminated earlier under Section 15.

14.2 At the end of the Initial Term, this Agreement renews automatically for successive renewal terms of twelve (12) months each (each a "**Renewal Term**"), unless either party gives the other written notice of non-renewal at least sixty (60) days before the end of the Initial Term or the then-current Renewal Term.

14.3 The terms of this Agreement, as amended from time to time, continue to apply in each Renewal Term, subject to any Fee increase made under Section 4.7.

## 15. Termination

15.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement with immediate effect by written notice if the other party: (a) commits a material breach of this Agreement and, if the breach can be remedied, fails to remedy it within thirty (30) days of receiving written notice of it; or (b) becomes insolvent, makes an assignment for the benefit of creditors, has a receiver or administrator appointed over any of its assets, or becomes subject to any similar proceeding.

15.2 Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience on not less than ninety (90) days' prior written notice to the other party.

15.3 Customer may terminate this Agreement by written notice if the Platform's availability falls below 99.0% in any two consecutive calendar months, or in any three calendar months in a period of twelve (12) months. Customer must give this notice within sixty (60) days of the end of the last month concerned.

15.4 If Customer terminates under Section 15.2, Customer will pay the Fees for the rest of the then-current Contract Year. If Customer terminates under Section 15.1 or 15.3, or Provider terminates under Section 15.2, Provider will refund any prepaid Fees for the period after the date of termination.

15.5 On termination or expiry of this Agreement: (a) Customer's right to use the Platform ends; (b) Section 8.7 and Section 9.6 apply; (c) each party will return or destroy the other's Confidential Information, except as required by law; and (d) Customer will pay all unpaid Fees due up to the date of termination.

15.6 Sections 4, 7.1, 8.3, 8.4, 8.7, 9.6, 10, 12, 13, 15.4 to 15.6, 16 and 21 continue after termination or expiry, together with any other provision that by its nature is intended to continue.

## 16. Insurance

16.1 During the Term and for two (2) years after it, Provider will maintain with reputable insurers: (a) commercial general liability insurance of at least USD 2,000,000 per occurrence; (b) professional liability (errors and omissions) insurance, including cyber liability, of at least USD 5,000,000 per claim; and (c) workers' compensation insurance as required by law.

16.2 On Customer's request, Provider will provide certificates of insurance showing that the insurance in Section 16.1 is in force.

## 17. Force majeure

17.1 Neither party is liable for any failure or delay in performing its obligations, other than payment obligations, to the extent caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including natural disasters, war, terrorism, riots, epidemics, government action, failures of public utilities or networks not caused by that party, and labour disputes not involving that party's own workforce (a "**Force Majeure Event**").

17.2 The affected party will notify the other party promptly, use reasonable efforts to mitigate the effect of the Force Majeure Event, and resume performance as soon as reasonably possible.

17.3 If a Force Majeure Event prevents a party from performing its material obligations for more than sixty (60) consecutive days, either party may terminate this Agreement by written notice, and Provider will refund any prepaid Fees for the period after termination.

## 18. Compliance

18.1 Each party will comply with all anti-bribery, anti-corruption, export control and sanctions laws that apply to it in connection with this Agreement.

18.2 Provider will not export Customer Data, or allow access to it from, any country that is subject to comprehensive US or EU sanctions.

## 19. Assignment and change of control

19.1 Neither party may assign or transfer this Agreement without the other party's prior written consent, which may not be unreasonably withheld. However, either party may assign this Agreement without consent to an Affiliate or to a successor to all or substantially all of its business or assets to which this Agreement relates, provided it gives the other party written notice.

19.2 If Provider undergoes a change of control in favour of a direct competitor of Customer, Customer may terminate this Agreement by written notice within ninety (90) days of being told of the change of control, and Provider will refund any prepaid Fees for the period after termination.

## 20. Notices

20.1 Notices under this Agreement must be in writing and delivered by hand, by courier, or by email to the addresses set out in the Order Form, or to any other address a party notifies to the other for this purpose.

20.2 A notice is treated as received: (a) if delivered by hand or courier, on delivery; or (b) if sent by email, at the time of transmission, unless the sender receives a delivery failure message. A notice received after 5:00 p.m. or on a day that is not a Business Day is treated as received on the next Business Day.

20.3 A notice of breach, termination or non-renewal sent by email must also be sent by courier within two (2) Business Days.

## 21. Governing law and disputes

21.1 This Agreement, and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it or its subject matter or formation (including non-contractual disputes or claims), is governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of New York, without regard to its conflict of laws rules.

21.2 The parties will first try to resolve any dispute through good-faith negotiation between their senior executives for a period of thirty (30) days after either party gives written notice of the dispute.

21.3 Any dispute that is not resolved under Section 21.2 shall be finally resolved by arbitration under the Rules of the London Court of International Arbitration. The seat of the arbitration is London, England. The tribunal consists of three arbitrators, and the language of the arbitration is English.

21.4 Nothing in this Section 21 prevents either party from seeking urgent injunctive relief from any court of competent jurisdiction.

21.5 The United Nations Convention on Contracts for the International Sale of Goods does not apply to this Agreement.
