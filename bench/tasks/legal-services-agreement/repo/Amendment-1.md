# AMENDMENT No. 1 TO THE MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Amendment No. 1 (the "**Amendment**") is made on 15 September 2025 between Kestrel Data Systems, Inc. ("**Provider**") and Halvorsen Freight Holdings, Inc. ("**Customer**").

## Background

A. The parties signed a Master Services Agreement dated 1 March 2024 (the "**Agreement**").

B. Customer has added two depots in Poland and asked for more flexibility to end the Agreement as its fleet plans change. In return, Customer has agreed to a fixed limit on each party's liability.

## Terms

1. **Definitions.** Terms defined in the Agreement have the same meaning in this Amendment.

2. **Limitation of liability.** Section 12.1 of the Agreement is deleted and replaced with the following:

   "12.1 Except as set out in Sections 12.2 and 12.3, each party's total aggregate liability arising out of or in connection with this Agreement, whether in contract, tort (including negligence), breach of statutory duty or otherwise, shall not exceed one million eight hundred thousand US dollars (USD 1,800,000)."

3. **Termination for convenience.** In Section 15.2 of the Agreement, the notice period for termination for convenience by Customer is changed from ninety (90) days to forty-five (45) days. The notice period for termination for convenience by Provider stays ninety (90) days.

4. **New Customer Sites.** The Warsaw PL depot and the Gdansk PL depot are added as Customer Sites. Provider will deliver and install four (4) additional Appliances at each of them by 31 October 2025 at the replacement cost and annual rental rate per Appliance in Order Form No. 1.

5. **Effect.** This Amendment takes effect on the date it is signed. Except as set out in it, the Agreement continues in full force and effect. This Amendment forms part of the Agreement.
